So although we cannot diagnose or prescribe, we teach each other awareness.

There is a second reason we believe doctors are threatened. They need to maintain the elitist barrier between patient and physician and uphold their mystic powers. It is essential for them to hoard and guard their knowledge and to keep us ignorant. We've been told so many times "it won't hurt", "you can't really feel that way", "it's not serious", etc. that we begin to doubt ourselves rather than the doctor. They see us gaining power over our lives;

power which we must take from them. It's simply not supposed to be our jurisdiction to know or care about our own bodies. This kind of paranoia clearly shows in the recent bust at the L.A. Feminist Women's Health Center. Two women were arrested for teaching self health to other women. It is incredible that these men could try to stop us from taking control of what should naturally belong to us.

When we begin questioning what have been traditional practices for generations (such as forced sterilization or unnecessary removal of reproductive genital organs) and when we begin demanding gentleness, knowledge and control (self-help, patient advocacy, abortion on demand) then they show up with force (i.e. arrest) that it will not be tolerated.

Women must show solidarity; we must continue to learn more about all areas of our own health; we must teach each other whatever skills we learn. We in Colorado Springs are ready to do whatever is necessary to spread the word. The L.A. Feminist Women's Health Center

sums it up beautifully.

The concept of self-help stresses SISTERHOOD that makes possible the benefits from collective knowledge, collective experience, collective training, and especially the sisterly concern for one another. When it is necessary to travel for more extensive experience or information, we do. Lastly, the self-help concept emphasizes competent medical back-up and the use of safe equipment at all times.

We want to hear from sisters in the Rocky Mountain Region. Are you involved in women's health care, are you interested in further information, are you now working or ready to work in the women's health movement? Contact us, Colorado Springs Women's Health Collective by mail at 730 N. Tejon 80903. We will cooperate with you in furthering the revolutionary cause of women's health and the women's movement.
