We are a collective of eigh
women functioning as a world
wide conspiracy of feisty
females.

We talked about men and women today.
It seems there's a new theory out.
Did you know Man's a million years old?
And during those million years
Woman served in some strange capacity.
Yes, they did.
"Man-hunting".
And because woman had babies
Who couldn't climb,
She became basically dependent for a long time.
And dependent's what she is today.
So she stayed home and made a nest
And gathered food to bring to the cave.

Okay.  Maybe so.
But the ways that women have evolved in this pattern.
Doesn't mean politically set up is the best,
And feather a nest
and raise young.
For all time.
Evolution, biology,
Stacking up on blind
Tradition can't repress oppression
Proofing beyond a shadow of a doubt
That women are "different".
That woman are more "passive".
That women are "gatherers".
Never argue with a biologist.

UGH!
If this is so,
Then why is my soul screaming, "no, no, no!"
When I look at the stars at night,
Or a distant mountain top
And yearn to fly;
Have my own adventure; climb;
To find my place in the universe;
To leave the nest far behind
Like a caterpillar to a butterfly--
Why?

WHY NOT!
