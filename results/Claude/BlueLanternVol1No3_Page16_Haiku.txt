ANNOUNCEMENTS

Women & the Law Conference

This year's national conference will
be held at the University of Wisconsin School during March 24-27.
Speakers include Rep. Elizabeth Holtzman, Professor
Bryna Hill Kay, and feminist singer
Margie Adam.

The conference will have close to
30 workshops focusing on such subjects as Lesbian Rights, Third World
Women, Careers, Women in Health, the
ERA, Labor Unions and Women in Prison.

For further information, write:
Women & the Law Conference, Univ. of
Wisconsin, Madison, Wisconsin
Consin. 53706

The Association for Women's Concern of the University of Virginia is sponsoring a symposium on "Women and Work" on March 25 in the New Cabell Hall. Featured speakers for discussion will include the work situation for women and the directions women must go to eliminate "pink collar ghettoes".

The Women's Alliance at the University of Virginia is soliciting signatures from students in an effort to obtain more women faculty members at the university. For information, call
Mary, 296-1240.
