Women & the Law Conference
This year's national conference will be held at the University of Wisconsin Law School during March 24-27. Featured speakers are: Elaine Noble, Rep. Elizabeth Holtzman, Professor Herma Hill Kay, and feminist singer Margie Adam. 
The conference will have close to 30 sessions dealing with such subjects as Human Rights, Third World Women, Abortion and Health, the ERA, Labor Unions and Women in Prisons/further information: 
Women & the Law Conference, Univ. of Wis. Law School, Madison, Wisconsin, 53706
Concerns of the University of Virginia is sponsoring a symposium on "Women and Work" on March 25 in the Newcomb Hall Ballroom at U. Va.. Topics for discussion will include the work situation for women and the directions women must go to eliminate "pink collar ghettos". 
The Women's Alliance at the University of Virginia is soliciting signatures from students in an effort to obtain more women faculty members at the University. For information, call Margy, 296-1210.

The Women's Resource and Services Center, a cooperative project of the YMCA and Total Action Against Poverty, is planning a Women's Art Festival. The festival, planned for March 6-12, will be a celebration for women in the Roanoke area to commemorate our 2nd century.
The celebration theme is Artemis, a variety of events is planned. 
Activities will include: visual arts showing, a publication of poetry, short plays, music, skit performances and panel discussions, and drama presentations. 
If interested in participating contact Mrs. Nolan, 342-4076, 343-6016, 345-3947.

Gay Conference
The Second Annual Southeastern Gay Conference will be held at U.N.C. at Chapel Hill, April 1-3. It is the only regional gay conference of this size, scope, and focus, and is organized by the Carolina Gay Association. Speakers include: Del Martin and Phyllis Lyon, Karla Jay, Allen Young, among others. Cost of the conference is $15 if you register before March 1, $6 student, $8 regular. After that it is $18, $8, $10. For more information and registration forms, write: Carolina Gay Association, Subsidiary Box 39, J.S.O.U.N.C., Chapel Hill, N.C. 27514, (919-942-7098/919-942-2039)
