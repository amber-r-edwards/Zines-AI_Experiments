He talked about men and women today.
It seems there's a new theory out.
Did you know man is a million years old?
And, during those million years
Men and women developed differently.
Men, they did.
Yes, but, what about--
Because men had babies,
And because women had babies
Who couldn't help
Being so helpless and dependent for a long time,
She couldn't hunt with the men.
So the safe, quiet, place and a nest,
And the woman had to bring it to her.

Okay.  Maybe so.
But he says that women have evolved in this pattern.
They are genetically set up to stay home and raise young.
Faithful, quiet,
Resting, no mind
No reasoning,
but slow biological progression
And women, because of a doubt
about other "different".
Men are "futurant".
Women are "static".
They are "static".

But
If this is so, I am still screaming, "no, no, no!"
Why do I look at the stars at night,
Or a distant mountain top
with strange compulsion
To find my place in the universe;
To reach, outward and behind
To carve out a stairway to infinity--
Why?
